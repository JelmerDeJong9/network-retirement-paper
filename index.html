Network Effects of Increases in the Statutory Pension Age

Jelmer de Jong · Daniël van Vuuren · Marike Knoef
Tilburg University, Department of Economics
Preliminary draft — October 2026.
This paper is work in progress. An updated working-paper version will be posted here when available.

Abstract

We study whether increases in the statutory pension age spill over through workplace and family networks. Using Dutch administrative data and policy-induced variation in peers' pension eligibility, we estimate how peers' retirement affects individual retirement and labor supply.
